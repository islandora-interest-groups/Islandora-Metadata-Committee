* Discussion: XML
  * Maybe it's being put out to pasture. Mike guest-lectured on XML and felt like "Ol' Pappy telling a story." Not really a need to think about it since the migration to Islandora Modern.
  * We still think of it (often MODS or VRA Core) when designing metadata profiles for our objects.
  * Rosie demo'ed the MODS and DC XML options that exist in the sandbox and can exist in your repository (though in many cases doesn't). They're populated by a View, which pulls information out of the item, and then that's formatted through a template in the REST OAI-PMH module. 
  * Next time: AI for Metadata Enhancement
  * Mike is publishing an article about it!
* Open Conversation
  * Rosie: The [RTPP project](https://respectfulterminology.ca/) (Canada) is coordinating the custodianship of vocabularies from various Indigenous groups. They'll probably have URIs but we're not sure if our taxonomy terms will be good enough at modelling them. Mike likened this to "Spanish, Castilian" as an example of a qualified subject term that makes things more confusing.
